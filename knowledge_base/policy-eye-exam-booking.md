# Eye Exam Booking

## About the eye exam

We offer a **free eye exam** for all customers, with no purchase required. The exam combines a computerized measurement with a manual (subjective) refraction test and is performed by a certified Refraksionis Optisien (RO). It takes about **20 to 30 minutes**. You receive a printed copy of your prescription at no charge.

The eye exam measures your vision for glasses and contact lenses. It is not a medical examination for eye diseases. If our staff notice signs of a possible eye condition, we will refer you to an ophthalmologist (dokter spesialis mata).

## How to book

- **Website:** fill in the booking form and choose a branch, date, and time.
- **WhatsApp:** send a message to the branch you want to visit.
- **Walk-in:** walk-in customers are welcome, but customers with a booking are served first.

To reschedule or cancel, use the WhatsApp link in your booking confirmation at least 2 hours before your appointment.

## What to bring

- Your current glasses.
- Your contact lens box or contact lens prescription, if you wear contact lenses.
- Any previous prescription.

If you wear soft contact lenses, please do not wear them for at least 2 hours before the exam, so that the measurement is accurate.

## Children

Children aged 5 and older can have an eye exam in store and must be accompanied by a parent or guardian. Children under 5 are referred to an ophthalmologist.

## Prescriptions from elsewhere

We accept prescriptions from ophthalmologists and other optical stores that were issued within the last 12 months. Older prescriptions need a new eye exam.

## Contact lens fitting

A contact lens fitting is a separate appointment of about 45 minutes. For first-time wearers it includes training on how to insert, remove, and care for the lenses.
