# Frame Materials

## Plastic frames

- **Acetate (cellulose acetate):** A plant-based plastic available in many colours and patterns, including layered and tortoiseshell looks. Durable and hypoallergenic, and can be adjusted with gentle heat. Slightly heavier than other plastics.
- **TR90 (thermoplastic nylon):** Very light and flexible, and bends without breaking. A good choice for active people and children. It can be adjusted less than acetate.
- **Injection-moulded plastic:** The most affordable option. Less adjustable and more likely to crack over time.

## Metal frames

- **Stainless steel:** Strong, light, corrosion resistant, and generally hypoallergenic.
- **Monel:** A common, affordable nickel-copper alloy. It can cause skin reactions in people with a nickel allergy.
- **Titanium:** Very light, very strong, corrosion resistant, and hypoallergenic. A premium material, and comfortable for all-day wear.
- **Beta-titanium:** A titanium alloy that is more flexible, often used for temples.
- **Memory metal:** A flexible alloy that bends back to its original shape after twisting.
- **Aluminium:** Light, strong, and often used for modern designs.

## Nickel allergy

Customers with a nickel allergy should choose titanium, stainless steel, or acetate frames, and avoid monel and other nickel alloys.

## Tropical climate

Heat, humidity, and sweat can corrode some metal frames and loosen screws over time. Titanium, stainless steel, and acetate hold up well in a tropical climate. Nose pads collect sweat and oil and should be cleaned regularly.

## Natural materials

Wood and buffalo horn frames are unique and light but are more delicate, less adjustable, and need more careful maintenance.
