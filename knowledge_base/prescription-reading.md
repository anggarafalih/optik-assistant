# How to Read an Eyeglass Prescription

## Which eye

- **OD** (oculus dexter) means the right eye.
- **OS** (oculus sinister) means the left eye.
- **OU** (oculus uterque) means both eyes.

Prescriptions in Indonesia often use **R** and **L**, or **Kanan** (right) and **Kiri** (left), instead.

## The numbers

- **SPH (sphere):** The main lens power, in diopters (D). A minus sign (−) means nearsightedness (myopia, "mata minus"). A plus sign (+) means farsightedness (hyperopia, "mata plus"). The larger the number, the stronger the prescription. "PL", "plano", or 0.00 means no power.
- **CYL (cylinder):** The power that corrects astigmatism ("mata silinder"). If this box is empty, there is no astigmatism correction.
- **AXIS:** A number from 1 to 180 degrees that gives the orientation of the astigmatism correction. It only appears with a CYL value. The axis is a direction, not a strength.
- **ADD (addition):** Extra plus power for near vision, added on top of the sphere for reading, bifocal, or progressive lenses. It usually ranges from +0.75 to +3.00 and is normally the same for both eyes.
- **PRISM and BASE:** Prism power, with a direction (BU = base up, BD = base down, BI = base in, BO = base out), used to help with eye alignment problems. Prism is uncommon.
- **PD (pupillary distance):** The distance between the centres of the pupils, in millimetres. Adults typically measure between 54 and 74 mm. It can be one number (for example 62) or two numbers, one per eye (for example 31/31). The PD is used to centre the lenses. If it is not on your prescription, our staff will measure it.

## Example

```
OD: SPH −2.50  CYL −0.75  AXIS 180
OS: SPH −2.25  CYL −1.00  AXIS 170
ADD +1.50      PD 62
```

This person is nearsighted in both eyes (−2.50 right, −2.25 left), has mild astigmatism in both eyes, needs +1.50 extra power for reading (so progressive or bifocal lenses may suit them), and has a pupillary distance of 62 mm.

## Glasses prescription vs. contact lens prescription

A glasses prescription cannot be used directly to buy contact lenses. A contact lens prescription also includes the base curve (BC), the diameter (DIA), and the lens brand. For prescriptions stronger than about ±4.00, the contact lens power is also different from the glasses power, because contact lenses sit directly on the eye. A contact lens fitting is needed.

## How long a prescription is valid

Vision can change over time. A new eye exam is recommended every 1 to 2 years for most adults, and every year for children and people with diabetes.
