# Photochromic (Light-Adaptive) Lenses

Photochromic lenses are clear indoors and darken automatically outdoors when exposed to ultraviolet (UV) light. They are sometimes called light-adaptive or transition lenses. One pair of glasses can work as both regular glasses and sunglasses.

## How fast they change

- Darkening: usually within about 30 seconds to 1 minute in sunlight.
- Clearing: fading back takes longer, typically about 3 to 5 minutes to become mostly clear, and longer to become completely clear.

## Temperature affects darkness

Photochromic lenses get darker in cold weather and less dark in hot weather. In hot, tropical conditions they may not become as dark as regular sunglasses.

## Driving and cars

Most photochromic lenses do not darken much inside a car, because the windshield blocks most UV light. Some special variants are designed to darken partially behind a windshield. For driving in bright sun, dedicated sunglasses (ideally polarized) are recommended.

## Other facts

- Most photochromic lenses block 100% of UVA and UVB light in both their clear and dark states.
- They are available in single vision and progressive designs and in most lens materials.
- Common colours are grey and brown, with some green options.
- Most photochromic lenses are not polarized.
- Their response may gradually become slower or less dark after several years of use.
