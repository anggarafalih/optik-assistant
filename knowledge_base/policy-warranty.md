# Warranty Policy

## Frames

Frames are covered for **12 months** from the date of purchase against manufacturing defects under normal use, such as broken solder joints, faulty hinges, or plating that peels off.

## Lenses

Lenses are covered for **6 months** from the date of purchase against coating defects, such as a coating that peels or cracks without heat damage or misuse.

## What the warranty does not cover

- Accidental damage, such as dropping, sitting on, or stepping on the glasses.
- Scratches from normal wear or improper cleaning.
- Heat damage, such as coating cracks (crazing) from leaving glasses in a hot car.
- Loss or theft.
- Glasses that have been repaired or modified by another shop.
- Normal wear of nose pads and temple tips.

## How to make a warranty claim

1. Bring the glasses and your proof of purchase (paper receipt or digital invoice) to any of our branches.
2. Our staff will inspect the glasses.
3. If the claim is approved, we will repair the item or replace it with the same or a similar model.

Warranty repairs and replacements take **7 to 14 working days**.

## Free services at any time

Frame adjustments, cleaning, and screw tightening are free at any branch for glasses bought from us, with no time limit.
